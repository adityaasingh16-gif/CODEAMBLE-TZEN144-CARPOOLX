CarpoolX
Version 1.0

CarpoolX connects drivers with empty seats to passengers heading the same way, matching routes automatically so a trip can be shared instead of taken alone.

  • Route matching Rides are matched by comparing a passenger's pickup and drop-off against a driver's actual route, not just the start/end points.
  • Fair, shared fares The base fare splits across everyone on the ride, with a small detour charge only for the extra distance a pickup or drop-off adds.
  • Safety first Vehicle and license verification for drivers, in-app SOS alerts, and ride ratings after every trip.
  • Need something else? For troubleshooting and step-by-step help, visit the Help & Support page.
