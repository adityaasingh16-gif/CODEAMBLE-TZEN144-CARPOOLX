Project Description

CarpoolX is an intuitive, modern ride-sharing web application built to connect commuters, reduce carbon footprints, and make daily city travel efficient and affordable.


Featuring interactive OpenStreetMap integrations, real-time seat pickers, light/dark themes, and safety-focused emergency controls.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a shared ride?
Enter your pickup and drop-off locations, select the required seats, and click 'Find Shared Rides'.
Is CarpoolX safe?
Yes! All drivers undergo background verification. You also have an emergency SOS auto-alert feature in settings.
How is the fare calculated?
Fares are split evenly between all co-riders based on distance and route duration.
