Driver Terms & Conditions

  1. Driver must maintain a valid commercial or relevant driving license at all times.
  2. Vehicle must hold valid fitness, registration (RC), and active third-party insurance.
  3. Strict adherence to dynamic carpool route schedules without unauthorized delays.
  4. Absolute zero tolerance for driving under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs.
  5. Driver must maintain car cleanliness and operational safety mechanisms (seatbelts, airbags).
  6. Carrying passengers exceeding permissible seating capacity is strictly illegal.
  7. Driver agrees to dynamic fair-share route pooling pricing calculated by CarpoolX algorithm.
  8. Emergency contacts and personal identity documents must remain up-to-date.
  9. Driver must follow national traffic laws and safe speed limits at all times.
  10. Respect passenger privacy and safety; improper behavior leads to immediate blacklisting.
  11. App notifications and GPS location tracking must remain ON during active trips.
  12. Vehicle images uploaded must match the real-time vehicle deployed for rides.
  13. Promptly report all road accidents or safety incidents to CarpoolX support immediately.
  14. Maintain transparent cashless transaction records through the platform.
  15. CarpoolX reserves the right to audit documents and vehicle status periodically.
