Need Help?

Use these quick links to move through the CarpoolX app. If you have any questions, the pages below explain common actions and let you continue your ride experience.

  • Login - Return here to sign in and access the dashboard.
  • Profile - Edit your contact details, emergency settings, and ride preferences.
  • History - View your past rides and rebook frequent trips.
  • Bookings - Manage your current ride and track your booking status.
  • Post Ride - Share feedback after completing a ride and continue to your next trip.
